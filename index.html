What this is

Smart Money Kompakt is a small tool used by the channel owner to upload the channel's own educational videos to TikTok. The creator signs in with TikTok. The tool sends a finished video file to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is published automatically: the creator reviews the draft in the TikTok app and publishes it manually.

Status: in development. Not available to the public.
