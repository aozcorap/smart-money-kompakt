1. Who is responsible

The operator named in the Impressum.

2. What data is processed

  • TikTok access token issued when the creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit). It is used only to upload video files to the creator's inbox.
  • TikTok display name and ID (scope user.info.basic) to show which account is signed in.
  • Video files chosen by the creator for upload. They are sent to TikTok and are not kept by the tool after the upload.

The tool does not read the creator's videos, followers, comments or messages, and does not post comments.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed only to perform the upload requested by the creator (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of the requested service).

4. Storage and deletion

The access token is stored only as long as needed for uploads, and deleted when the creator revokes access or on request. No data is sold or shared with third parties, except that video files and the token are transmitted to TikTok to perform the upload.

5. Your rights

You may request information, correction or deletion of your data and complain to a supervisory authority. Contact: see the Impressum.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security & permissions, Apps and services).
